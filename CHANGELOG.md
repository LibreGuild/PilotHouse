# Changelog

What changed in each release, newest first. The full notes are on the [releases page](https://github.com/LibreGuild/PilotHouse/releases).

Each release also has **Merge notes** for organisations that build their own copy of PilotHouse ([docs/customizing.md](docs/customizing.md)): what to look at when merging it. These cover new `[features]` keys (new features arrive switched on), new or renamed keys in `brand/brand.toml`, changes to `crates/custom` or `Store::apply_track`, and moved files.

## Unreleased

- **The Bridge: every task, by what it needs from you.** The window opens on four columns, *Needs you*, *Working*, *Ready for review* and *Done*, with the chats of every workspace in them (and agent CLIs in terminals, and reviews being readied). A card says which workspace and agent, what the agent is doing, asks or said last; it opens the chat, *Review changes* opens it with what changed beside it, *Done* puts it away. Chips narrow it to one workspace; *New task* starts a chat where you say. Before, chats were listed by where they live, under each workspace in a list beside the screen, and the ones that needed something had to be found (the *Agents* pop-up listed them, behind a button). The Bridge takes the place of both. ⌘⇧E and ⌘⇧A show it; the status bar counts *N need you · N working · N to look at* from anywhere.
- **One column of icons, with a badge per workspace.** The Bridge, Reviews, then each workspace as its letter on its colour, with a dot for what goes on there and its menu on right-click; *Chats* (for chats that need no folder), **+** and Settings. The list beside it and the column of tool icons on the right are gone, and the room goes to the work. ⌃1–⌃9 (Alt+1–9) still open the first nine workspaces.
- **A workspace's screen is two panes: the chat and one result pane.** Over the result pane are the names *Changes* (what changed, to review and commit), *Preview* (the browser, or a phone), *Terminal* and *Files* (the folder tree with the editor beside it); the board, run configurations, tests, pull requests and the phone's log are under ⋯. The tool in front has the whole pane, so the browser, a phone and the board are no longer squeezed into a column. ⌘B folds the chat to a strip at the edge, ⌥⌘B hides the result pane, ⌘⇧J gives the result pane the window, and ⌘J shows the terminal and goes back. The line between the two panes drags, and stays where you leave it. **This replaces the dock**, where every tool was a tab that could be dragged anywhere: the tool you had in front, whether the pane was open, your chat and your open files come back as they were, but an arrangement of your own (a tool moved beside or under the chat, the bottom panel) does not.
- **Reviews is a reviewer's queue** (it was *GitHub*). A list of pull requests is grouped by what an agent has made of each: *Findings to read*, *Agents at work*, *Not started*, *Reviewed, nothing found*. **Tick the ones nobody looked at and start an agent on each at once**; every review runs on PilotHouse's own copy of the repository, only reads, and posts nothing until you do. A row follows its review (*Cloning acme/shop 12s*, then what the agent is doing) and so does a card on the Bridge. A pull request with findings opens on them, and *Check the fixes* shows on one the author pushed to since.
- **The title bar** names what is in front (*Bridge*, *Reviews*, the workspace, *Chats*) and holds the switches of what is shown there; Settings moved to the foot of the column of icons.
- **⌘N on the Bridge** starts a chat in the workspace it is narrowed to, else in the one last in front. A command that acts on a workspace (a new chat, a tool's shortcut) brings that workspace forward first, so nothing happens unseen behind the Bridge or Reviews.
- The command palette's names follow: *Show the Bridge*, *Show Reviews*, *Show or hide the chat*, *Show or hide the result pane*, *Let the result pane fill the window* (the old names still find them).

### Merge notes

- `ph_workspace::View` gained `Bridge` (the default); `View::Workspaces` now means *a workspace's screen is in front*. `ph_workspace::Section` and `Workspace::section()` are gone, with the lists they chose between. An organisation's views (`ph_custom::views`) show in the column of icons after Reviews, as before.
- Removed files: `crates/workspace/src/agents_board.rs` and `crates/workspace/src/workspace_screen/dock.rs`. New: `crates/workspace/src/bridge.rs`, `crates/workspace/src/workspace_screen/layout.rs`, `crates/workspace/src/github_view/queue.rs`. `sidebar.rs` keeps only the width of Reviews' list; `rail.rs` is the one column of icons.
- **A tool of your own beside the chat** no longer has a saved name in `dock.rs` (that step of [docs/customizing.md](docs/customizing.md) is gone). It needs its `ContextTab`, its action and handler, and its panel in `workspace_screen/panels.rs` and `render.rs`, as before; it shows under ⋯ over the result pane, and as a name there while in use. `ContextTab::in_bottom_panel` is gone.
- `ProjectUiState` (the JSON in `projects.ui_state`) gained `chat_folded`, `tree_open`, `preview` and `chat_share`; rows without them read as before. The settings `workspace.dock.<id>`, `workspace.sidebar_expanded` and `workspace.sidebar_folded` are no longer read (they are left in place); `workspace.front_view` is new.
- Actions keep their names, so your key bindings and menus hold: `ShowWorkspacesView` and `ShowAgentsView` show the Bridge, `ShowGitHubView` shows Reviews, `ToggleSidebar` folds the chat (in Reviews, its list), `ToggleBottomPanel` and `ToggleTerminal` show the terminal. The `github` feature key still switches Reviews off.
- Element ids that tests or scripts of yours may click changed: `view-workspaces` is `view-bridge`; the `sidebar-…` ids are gone (a workspace is `("rail-workspace", id)`, a task `bridge-card-<session>`); a result pane's names are `context-tab-changes`, `-preview`, `-terminal`, `-files` and `context-tab-<tool>`; `toggle-chat` and `unfold-chat` are new; an inbox row is `github-found-<host>/<owner>/<name>#<number>` (it was numbered by position). `app.state` reports `"view": "bridge"` for the Bridge.
- `ChatPanel::start_review_behind` is new (a review as a chat of its own behind the one in view); `PullsPanel::show_findings` now also holds for a pull request that is still opening.

## 0.6.4

- **The same look on macOS, Windows and Linux.** PilotHouse now carries its fonts, Inter for the interface and JetBrains Mono (without ligatures) for code, so text has the same shapes and takes the same room on every system; before, each system's own font made the same layout sit differently. *Settings → Appearance → Interface font* still offers the system's font and every installed family.
- **One interface size, 16, on every system** (it was 15 on macOS and Linux, 18 on Windows). At 16 every text size, row and gap is a whole pixel, also on a screen at 150 % or 200 %; the earlier sizes fell between pixels, which a Windows screen at 100 % showed as rows and paddings a pixel off. A size you chose yourself stays; the old defaults move to the new one, once.
- **Interface size scales everything**, as zooming a web page does: icons, agent marks, badges, indents, the file tree's rows, the chat's spacing and reading width, the tool icons' column and dialogs grow and shrink with the text. They had fixed pixel sizes before, so a larger text sat beside small icons in dialogs that were too narrow for it.
- **Workspaces, Chats and GitHub in a column of icons at the left edge**, like the tools' column on the right. Each shows its own list beside the screen in front: the workspaces, the chats outside any workspace (with the pinned ones), or GitHub's inbox and repositories. They shared the sidebar as two stacked panes before, which grew crowded. Pressing the icon in front hides or shows the list. Chats has a shortcut of its own, ⌘⇧D (Ctrl+Shift+D). The title bar names what is in front instead of holding the switch.
- **A frame with islands in it.** The title bar, the two columns of icons and the status bar are now one piece, with no line between them and a little light from above in the theme's own colour. What changes with what you look at, the list and the panes, sits in it as rounded panels with a small gap between them.
- **The frame takes the colour of the workspace in front**: faint, strongest in the title bar and fading down the two edges, with the workspace's dot before its name. A glance at the window's edge says where a new chat would go. Chats and GitHub belong to no workspace and stay plain.
- **New chat and Search chats lead the Chats list only.** Over the workspaces, *New chat* went to whichever workspace was in front without naming it; there a chat starts from the workspace's own row, the chat's header or ⌘N. Search from the Chats list covers every chat of every workspace; beside a workspace it starts with that workspace's chats.
- **Views fit the room they have.** A tool's row of views (Git's Changes, Branch, History and Stashes; a pull request's six tabs) folds the ones not in front to their icons in a narrow pane instead of cutting them off. Rows of named buttons (the board's, the Git list's) fold to icons, the commit row wraps, sentences wrap, and the empty chat's starters stack in one column.
- **Fixed: a narrow chat's starters could not be reached.** Beside a pull request (or at a large interface size) the cards of an empty chat stack in one column; taller than the pane, they were cut off at both ends. The chat's empty states now scroll.
- **Fixed: *Check again* at the bottom of the Pull requests tool** when a folder has no GitHub remote: it sits under the message now.
- **Fixed: the chat came back a sliver after *Reset layout* and a restart.** The saved layout held a guess of the pane sizes, not what was drawn. Layouts saved that way are put right when they are loaded.
- **Diffs are easier to read**, wherever they are listed (the Git tab, commits, stashes, a pull request's files, a chat's edits): both files' line numbers, `+` and `−` in a column of their own, and the words that changed in a replaced line marked more strongly. Long lines scroll sideways instead of being cut, also in a pull request's **Files** tab.
- **A review says at once that it has begun.** *Review with agent* opens the chat with the request and its first step, *Cloning owner/repo…* with the time it has taken, as part of the run; the Agents pop-up lists it as work. Before, a small line over the composer was all that showed, and for a pull request opened from the inbox nothing at all (its chat was not attached).
- **A pull request's page** has one button, *Review with agent*; Watch, Check out, Address comments and Refresh are in its ⋯ menu.
- **The editor's bar** holds the file's path and one ⋯ menu (find, symbols, ask the agent, blame) instead of a row of buttons.
- **Auto save** (Settings → Editor): after a pause in typing, or when you leave the editor. Off by default. It never writes what only you may decide (a file changed or removed on disk, text restored after a crash), and with it on, closing a tab writes the file instead of asking.
- **Fixed: two saves at once** (⌘S twice, or beside an auto save) could report a file as changed on disk, or on a slow drive leave it cut short. Saves now run one after the other.
- **Fixed: *Discard* of text restored after a crash** left the restored text standing.
- **Fixed: saving a file (⌘S) jumped to its start.** A file read again while it is open keeps the cursor and the scroll.
- **A new branch is always on offer in the branch picker**: type a name and press Enter (spaces become dashes; a name Git refuses says why).
- **Cline without `--acp`** (an organisation's own build, an old version): the chat says so and offers to run Cline in a terminal, where PilotHouse follows it like a chat.
- **A working mark of PilotHouse's own**, a small fluttering pennant, where a 12 px spinner used to go unnoticed.
- **Names in the command palette and the menus are complete**: no trailing "…", and *Show or hide the sidebar* rather than *Toggle sidebar* (the old names still find them).
- **Contrast:** muted text, filled buttons and links in Warm Light, One Dark, One Light and Studio Dark now reach WCAG AA (4.5:1) on every surface, as the Modern themes did.
- **Title bar:** in a narrow window or at a large interface size the search box gives way, and the buttons on the right (Inbox, Agents, the panel toggles, Settings) stay on screen. **Git:** in a narrow column the Commit button moves to a line of its own instead of past the panel's edge.

### Merge notes

- New files: `crates/theme/fonts/` (the font files and their licences, about 3.2 MB in the binary) and `crates/theme/src/fonts.rs`; `NOTICE` names them. A copy that wants its own fonts replaces the files and the names in `fonts.rs`.
- `Preferences::sizes_version` is now 2. A copy with its own default sizes changes `DEFAULT_SIZES` in `crates/theme/src/preferences.rs`; nothing there depends on the platform any more.
- An organisation's own view (`ph_custom::views`) is now drawn inside a rounded panel of the window's frame (`crates/workspace/src/frame.rs`), with the frame around it. A view that drew its own border, rounded corners or outer padding should drop them, or it shows a panel in a panel.
- Views in the main window sit in islands: `crate::frame::island(window, cx)` in `crates/workspace`, which is `ph_ui::Surface::new().behind(top, bottom)`. A plain `Surface` still works; its children then show square in its corners where the frame behind is another colour.
- `View` has no new variant: Workspaces and Chats are `Section`s of `View::Workspaces` (`Workspace::section`, `show_section`). An organisation's views (`ph_custom::views`) now show as icons in the left column (`crates/workspace/src/rail.rs`) instead of the title bar's switch; nothing changes in `crates/custom`.
- New in `ph_ui`: `Loader` (use it instead of gpui-component's `Spinner`), `PillTabs`, `measure::{width_of, narrower_than}`. `ph_preview::diff` draws every listed diff (`lines`, `line_row`, `scroller`).
- `EditorPreferences` has a new field, `auto_save`; a value this build does not know reads as off.
- The command palette's labels changed (articles added, "…" removed, *Toggle …* became *Show or hide …*). The old names are kept as keywords; a build that added entries of its own may want the same wording.
- Views size things with `ph_ui::rems_from_px` and `ph_ui::scaled_px` (new, `crates/ui/src/scale.rs`) rather than `px`. `ph_ui::file_icon_element`, `folder_icon_element`, `agent_logo`, `logo_mark`, `app_icon` and `AgentSpec::mark` take any length (`px(16.)` still compiles); `ChatWidth::max_width` returns rems. Your own crates keep working as they are; converting their `px` sizes makes them follow the interface size too.

## 0.6.3

- **Copy rendered Markdown as rich text.** Text selected in a Markdown document (the formatted editor and the preview) or in a chat reply is copied as plain text and as HTML, so it pastes into Confluence, a mail or a document with its headings, tables, lists and code, as from VS Code's Markdown preview (macOS and Windows).
- **Devices: mirroring no longer slows the phone.** While a phone was mirrored, PilotHouse asked it for its rotation every second (`dumpsys display`), which made the phone itself stutter. A turned screen is now seen in the video, and the phone is asked only then. A still screen also costs the computer less.
- **Files:** a strip under the tree stays free for the folder's menu and for drops, however many files there are; the tree is read again when you come back to the window (for folders whose changes the system does not report, such as network drives) and by a new **Refresh** button; it is drawn again only when a folder's listing changed.
- **A file opened in a window of its own** (from Explorer or Finder) has its **Edit** button for pictures and videos, and now PowerPoint decks: Studio opens without a workspace. The Markdown views and *Format* for JSON are there too.
- **Helper processes have names.** The MCP bridge of each agent session and the Antigravity bridge show as `pilothouse-mcp` and `pilothouse-agy-acp` in Task Manager and Activity Monitor, not as more copies of `pilothouse`.
- **Windows fixes:** *Show in Explorer* works for files and folders below the workspace's top folder (and copied paths use `\` throughout); Studio's title-bar buttons (**Export…**, undo, redo, ⋯) take clicks; a video export no longer ends with "access is denied" (the finished file is closed before it is moved, the move is tried again for a moment, and a file that is open elsewhere is left alone, the result saved beside it).
- **Browser:** a stopped browser says it is stopped, with **Start the browser**, instead of "Starting the browser…" with a spinner that never ended; the menu's Stop also stops a browser that is still starting. Agents are told that the built-in browser is driven with PilotHouse's own browser tools (an agent with another browser or REPL tool of its own tried that one, and failed).
- **Lighter while agents work.** The Git tool read the whole working tree (three `git` runs, one a full `git diff`) on every change to a file, shown or not, for every workspace with its Git tool opened once. It now does so only while it is on screen, at most every five seconds for the count on its icon, and not at all for a workspace that is not in front; reads no longer run back to back while files keep changing, and one that finds nothing new draws nothing. Git and the file in front are also read once when you come back to the window.
- **Lighter in the background.** With PilotHouse behind another program, the status bar's look at ports and CI runs once a minute instead of every ten seconds, and no longer starts `git` each time to see whether the commit changed. On Windows it started `netstat` twice and `tasklist` every ten seconds for ports it could never show there; it no longer does.
- **Fixed: the app's log lost to other runs.** Opening a file with PilotHouse from Explorer or Finder, the quick chat shortcut, `pilothouse --version` and `--help` each started a new log file, moving the running app's log along until what it had written was gone. Only the app itself starts its log now.
- **Fixed:** opening a tool from the icon bar while the Chat tab was in front no longer brings the Editor tab forward; the Agents pop-up scrolls when its list is longer than the window.

## 0.6.2

- **Files: drag and drop, as in VS Code.** Drag rows (or all the selected ones) onto a folder to move them, after a question you can turn off; hold ⌥ (Ctrl on Windows and Linux) to copy. A closed folder opens when the drag rests on it. Files from Finder or Explorer are copied in where they are dropped; a row dropped on the editor opens, and on a chat it is attached.
- **Chat: paste images.** ⌘V with a screenshot or a copied image on the clipboard attaches it to the message; files copied or dropped from the system attach too.
- **Terminal: selection and right-click menu.** A drag selection follows the pointer anywhere in the window, ends where the button is let go, and scrolls the history when held above or below the terminal. Right-click offers Copy, Paste, Select All, Find, Ask in Chat, Split Terminal and Clear.
- **Agents use your Chrome first** for web pages, `localhost` included, and the built-in browser only when the extension is not connected. The first time an agent or a UI test run uses a workspace's built-in browser since PilotHouse started, its Browser tab comes to the front, so you see the test going on.
- **Chrome extension: from Chrome to PilotHouse.** Always on your own action, and into a chat's message box (the chat on screen, a new chat or quick chat) for you to send:
  - **Send** the selection, the page as Markdown, a link, an image, a screenshot of an area, or an element you click (selector, styles, the source file a dev build names, and a picture), from the right-click menu, shortcuts (⌥⇧P, ⌥⇧S, ⌥⇧E) or the panel.
  - **Record a bug report** (the steps, a video with still frames, the page's console errors and failed requests) or **a workflow** (steps that agents can run again), with a bar in the page that says so.
  - **`@tabs`** in a chat attaches the text of the tabs you shared with agents.
  - **Buttons on GitHub, Jira and Linear** (off until you turn a site on): review a pull request in PilotHouse, start a chat from an issue or a failed run, add an issue to the board.
  - **Send a summary** made on your computer, where Chrome has its built-in summarizer.
- **The PilotHouse panel** in Chrome's side panel (the toolbar button): chats waiting for you, agents in Chrome with Stop and Continue, sharing the tab, sending, recording and the options. The toolbar icon counts chats waiting for you; Chrome notifications when you choose; a tab comes forward when an agent needs you to sign in or prove you are a person.
- **Debugging tools for agents in your Chrome:** `chrome_performance` (Core Web Vitals and what slows the page), `chrome_audit` (accessibility, SEO, best practices), `chrome_emulate` (device, network, CPU, colour scheme, place, time zone), `chrome_mock` (answer requests to test empty, failing and slow states), `chrome_memory` (heap, detached DOM nodes and listeners, compared for leaks), `chrome_errors` (stacks mapped through source maps to the workspace's files), `chrome_site_tools` (sites' own tools, WebMCP, in Chrome's early preview). Emulation and mocks end by themselves once the agent leaves the tab idle, or when you press Stop.
- **Workflows:** agents run them (`chrome_workflows`, `chrome_run_workflow`), stopping where a password or a person is needed. Settings → Chrome extension lists them, makes a Playwright test or a schedule of one, renames and deletes them.
- A managed Chrome whose policy keeps extensions' debugger out now says so clearly, and what to do instead.
- **Fixes from a review of all of this:** recordings keep no secrets (shown passwords, card numbers, tokens in addresses); page text reaches agents marked as untrusted; sends go to the window in front; dropped and pasted images go to agents in a format they take; moving files never takes a workspace folder along and leaves no tab on an old path; mocks, emulation and site tools clean up after themselves; the panel reconnects by itself and says *Connected* only once PilotHouse answers.
- **The built-in browser** no longer drops an agent's first page while it starts.
- **After updating**, reload the extension in `chrome://extensions` (it asks Chrome for more permissions now; nothing reaches a site without your click or your yes). It needs Chrome 118 or later.

### Merge notes

- `ph_agent::LiveSources` has a new field, `tabs` (`None` where there is no `@tabs`): a copy that builds `LiveSources` itself adds `tabs: None`.
- The extension's files are listed in `crates/browser/src/extension.rs` (`FILES`); a test fails when a file in `crates/browser/extension/` is missing there.

## 0.6.1

- **Builds for organisations.** Everything an organisation changes now lives in a few places of its own. [docs/customizing.md](docs/customizing.md) explains each:
  - `brand/` holds the name, ids, icons, features on or off, and update sources, read by the app and by the packaging scripts.
  - `crates/custom` holds the organisation's own views beside Workspaces and GitHub, its commands and its startup code.
  - `CUSTOM.md` holds the organisation's rules for coding agents.
- **Coding agents get context.** [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) (and `CLAUDE.md`) covers how the code is laid out and its rules, and a `merge-upstream` skill takes in a new PilotHouse release.
- An organisation's tables go on a migration track of their own (`Store::apply_track`), so they never take PilotHouse's numbers.
- Appearance preferences that a build does not know (a newer one's, or an organisation's) are kept when it saves.
- `scripts/package/icon.py` now makes every icon from one picture (it used to draw an old version of the icon).
- **A new Walkthrough for pull requests.** For the tech lead or manager who reviews a change: a briefing in plain words, written by an agent that read the change and the code around it. It covers what changes and why, how that part of the system works today, what is different after (with diagrams), how it is done, what it affects, where to look closely, how it is tested and what to ask the author. Its text can be selected and copied, and references to code open in the Files tab or on the web. It replaces the gist, file groups and tour, and the tour strip in the Files tab; a walkthrough made by an earlier version is asked for again.
- **Cline.** Cline's CLI joins Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and Antigravity, with your own install and sign-in (`cline --acp`): chats with its models and its Plan and Act modes, PilotHouse's MCP tools and yours, one-off jobs like commit messages, its sessions in the usage dashboard, and Cline in a terminal with its past sessions to resume. It runs as in your terminal: on a provider Cline uses there that needs no key (Vertex AI, Ollama), not only on a Cline account. Where Cline's ACP side falls short, PilotHouse works around it: attachments go as text, tool titles that are JSON read as words, a reopened chat is recapped to a new session, it neither updates itself nor leaves a background hub running, and an expired sign-in says so with a way to sign in again.
- **Quick chat.** A small chat window over every app, from anywhere: ⌥Space on macOS, Alt+Shift+Space on Windows and Linux (set it in Settings → Chat), the command palette, or `pilothouse quick-chat` for a shortcut in the system's own settings (the only way on Wayland). It moves, resizes and comes back where it was, with the chat it showed; its chats are under Chats. [docs/features/quick-chat.md](docs/features/quick-chat.md)
- **Agents in a terminal stay in the chat list.** A coding agent's CLI run in a workspace terminal is saved as a chat once it is asked something. With its terminal open, clicking it shows the terminal; once closed, it shows the agent's last answer under its title, its session read only in the chat column, with *Continue in terminal* (the CLI resumes it) and *Continue in a new chat*, and Search chats finds what was said in it. Closing a terminal whose agent is at work or waiting for you asks first. Cline in a terminal is followed too, its transcript readable. Cline's provider can be chosen in the chat (⋯ → Provider): its accounts, and the providers saved with `cline auth` that need no key.
- **Clearer agent errors.** When an agent fails a request with a reason (Cline's "Internal error" carries one), the chat shows the reason. A sign-in that expires mid-chat offers the agent's sign-in, and a chat whose agent could not resume its session (a failed load) now gives the new session the chat's history. A remembered model the agent no longer offers is not asked for again.
- **Diagrams zoom.** Hovering a Mermaid diagram, in a chat, a pull request's walkthrough or a Markdown document, shows buttons to zoom out, zoom in, fit, open it large in a dialog and copy its source; a pinch or ⌘/Ctrl and the wheel zoom too.
- **Lighter while agents work.** An agent working in a terminal no longer redraws every window four times a second, nor writes to the database each second; the sidebar shares its chat lists instead of copying them on each draw, and the Chats list shows its 50 newest (*Show all* for the rest). A closed terminal's session file is not looked for again and again, a quick chat window saves where it is once it stops moving, and Cline's settings are read once per change, without waiting for the login shell.
- **Fixed: a database backup of one kind deleting the other.** Backups made before a migration and those made before an organisation's own migrations are now kept apart, three of each.
- **Fixed: Cline's provider changed in one workspace** now starts Cline again in every workspace, so none keeps running on the old one.
- **Fixed: agent processes left running.** A new chat that was replaced before its first message, or switched to another agent, left its agent session open until PilotHouse quit. The same happened to deleted chats and to chats let go of as idle. For Claude Code that is a process of its own with its MCP servers, hundreds of MB each. Sessions now close with their chat (`session/close`, where the agent offers it), and *New chat* on a still untouched chat keeps it.

### Merge notes

- First release with `brand/` and `crates/custom/`. If your copy already renamed PilotHouse in the code, move the name, ids and icons to `brand/brand.toml` and `brand/icons/` and take PilotHouse's code as it is.
- The icons moved to `brand/icons/` (`app.icns`, `app.ico`, `app-1024.png`, `app-256.png`, `title-bar.png`). The update key moved to `brand/update_key.pub`.
- `AgentSpec` has two new fields, `env` and `quirks`: an agent your copy added to `AGENTS` needs `env: &[]` and `quirks: Quirks::NONE`.
- `[features]` keys: `files`, `changes`, `pulls`, `board`, `browser`, `devices`, `runs`, `tests`, `terminal`, `logcat`, `github`, `schedules`, `utilities`, `usage`, `network_drives`, `parallel_runs`, `presentations`, `chrome_extension`, `quick_chat` (new in this release).
- Installer file names now start with the brand's name without spaces. They are unchanged for PilotHouse: `PilotHouse-macOS-universal.dmg` and the rest.
