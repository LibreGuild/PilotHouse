How it works

A task in four moves.

01

Ask

Say what you want. Point at things with @.

02

It works

It plans, edits, and asks before it runs anything.

03

You review

Read the diff. Keep or undo any part of it.

04

You ship

Commit, push, open the pull request. Same window.

Explain

An agent wrote it. You still understand it.

Before you commit, read the change in plain words. Flip the switch.

  • It stays with the commit.

    Weeks later, History still has the story.

  • Why is this here?

    Right-click any line. See who asked for it, and why.

  • Share it when you choose.

    One click adds it to your commit as a file. Never by itself.

The Bridge

Every task, sorted by what it needs from you.
